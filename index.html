Vancouver Night Sky

Modeled artificial sky brightness across the Lower Mainland, Sea-to-Sky and Sunshine Coast. Click any square kilometre for its statistics.

Method

Modeled zenith sky brightness from Falchi et al. (2016), derived from VIIRS/DNB radiances and resampled to a 269 × 191 grid at 1 km in BC Albers (EPSG:3005). Every per-cell statistic in the side panel follows from \(S\) alone.

Sky surface brightness
\( S = \text{offset} + \text{raw} \times \text{scale} \quad [\text{mag\,arcsec}^{-2}] \)

\( \text{offset} = 16.546, \quad \text{scale} = 8.32 \times 10^{-5} \)

Naked-eye limiting magnitude
\( N = 7.93 - 5\log_{10}\left(10^{4.316 - S/5} + 1\right) \)
Light pollution ratio
\( \text{ratio} = \frac{L(S)}{L_{\text{nat}}} \qquad L(S) = L_0 \cdot 10^{-S/2.5} \)
Visible star count
\( \text{count} \approx \text{round}\left(7.5 \cdot 3.1^{N-1}\right) \)
